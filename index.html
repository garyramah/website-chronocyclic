A foundation, not a feature.

Foundational layers get foundational names.
Chronocyclic reads like what it is: a tier-one photonics foundry and IP holding entity for the time-frequency layer of quantum computing.

Time lenses

Electro-optic systems that map time into frequency and back — the lens of the time-frequency world. They rotate states in chronocyclic space, enabling measurement and manipulation that no static optic can perform.

Biphoton dispersion physics

Entangled photon pairs born with time-frequency correlations, shaped by dispersive elements. The pair source is the workhorse; dispersion engineering is how its correlations are tuned, stretched, and put to use.

Time-frequency logical qubits

Qubits — and higher-dimensional qudits — encoded natively in time and frequency rather than bolted onto two-level systems. Hardware-native encoding means fewer conversions, fewer loss points, and a direct path to high-dimensional processing.

IP, held at the substrate

Component designs, source architectures, and shaping methods, held as a portfolio at the layer every time-frequency system shares. Builders license the substrate; they don't reinvent it.

The telemetry plane

The software layer that watches quantum states the way a control room watches a reactor: continuously, quantitatively, and before anything irreversible happens.

01

Watch Wigner negativity

Negativity in the Wigner function is the witness of nonclassicality — the part no classical light field can imitate. The plane tracks it live as a health metric of the state, not as a post-experiment curiosity.

02

Track phase-space correlations

The stripe's shape, tilt, and drift are monitored continuously. Correlation is the resource; watching it decay is watching the computation degrade, in time to do something about it.

03

Enforce boundary conditions

States that fail their checks do not enter the execution environment. Deterministic gates at the boundary — verified, logged, and policy-driven — instead of alarms after the fact.

04

Control plane & audit trail

Real-time dashboards, alerting, and a complete log of every state checked, every gate passed or failed, and every boundary enforced. Diagnostics you can show an auditor, not just an engineer.

From source to execution, gated.

Four stages. Nothing reaches execution unverified.

1

Generate

Biphoton sources produce time-frequency entangled states — the raw material of the computation.

2

Shape

Time lenses and dispersive elements shear, rotate, and focus states in chronocyclic space, preparing the exact correlations the computation needs.

3

Verify

The telemetry plane measures Wigner negativity and phase-space correlations against policy bounds. A state outside bounds is held, flagged, and logged — never silently passed.

4

Execute

Only verified states enter the execution environment. The boundary is where quality is enforced, and the log is where it is proven.
